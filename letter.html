Something I never said properly

I don't know exactly when it happened.

Maybe it was during all those conversations.

Maybe it was that night.

Maybe it happened slowly, while I wasn't paying attention.

But somewhere along the way, you stopped being just my best friend.

You became someone I wanted to choose.
Someone I wanted to keep.
Someone I didn't want to lose again.

And somewhere along the way, I started noticing all those little things about you that I probably never told you enough.

You are one of the most beautiful souls I have ever encountered in my life. Those beautiful eyes of yours make me feel like I could spend a lifetime looking into them and still wish for just one more moment.

You make me wonder how someone can be so humble and elegant at the same time, how someone can be so sweet and yet have that little bit of bitterness that somehow makes you even more adorable.

There is something about you that I can't quite put into words. Maybe it's the way you smile, the way you talk, the way you get annoyed over the smallest things, or the way you exists.

I love those little things about you that you probably don't even notice. It's not just your beautiful eyes, your cute nose, or that sweet smile of yours. I like those little gaps between your teeth when you smile, the way you squint ur eyes when your vision gets blurry, even that random little pimple you probably hate. I don't know why, but I find all of those things cute too.. I don't just like the perfect version of you. I like all the little imperfect, silly, complicated parts of you too. They’re what make you, you.

Somewhere along the way, you became someone I could talk to for hours, stare at for way too long, and still somehow want to spend a little more time with.

I know things are complicated right now.

I know you're angry with me because I kept asking you for an answer you weren't ready to give me. And I understand why that hurt you.

But I want you to know what was going through my head in that moment. I wasn't trying to force you because I wanted to hear the answer I wanted. I asked because, for a moment, I genuinely started feeling like maybe everything between us had been real only to me.

I started wondering if maybe you didn't feel anything for me at all, and you were only trying to protect the friendship between us — the same way you once told me you used to feel during my relationship with Pari.

And that thought honestly scared me.

I think I became so afraid of losing something that had started meaning so much to me that I desperately wanted to know whether I was imagining all of it.

That doesn't make it right that I pressured you for an answer. I should have trusted you enough to let you tell me when you were ready.

I'm genuinely sorry for putting you in that position.

I don't want you to say anything because I asked you to.
I don't want you to choose me because I made something beautiful.
And I definitely don't want you to feel like you owe me anything because of what we have shared.

I just wanted you to know what you mean to me.

That's all.

Whatever happens next, I'm grateful that somehow, after everything, we found our way back to each other.

After a year of not talking, somehow we ended up here — with all these memories, all these conversations, all these little moments that I never thought we'd have again.

And whatever this becomes, I don't want to forget how special that is.

— Kaddu
